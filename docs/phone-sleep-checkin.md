# Sleep without a wearable

Open Today → Log sleep. Choose **I don’t use a wearable** to see the fallback. The preference is saved to the authenticated account, defaults to UNKNOWN, and can be changed on the same screen. Unknown and wearable accounts never show the new phone estimate or rest/soreness controls. Missing Health Connect/HealthKit records do not infer absence of a wearable: neither platform can reliably enumerate every connected band. The choice is the explicit gate, not Bluetooth scanning.

Android offers a separately opted-in **Estimate my sleep** switch. Google's Sleep API uses device motion and ambient light and requires Physical activity permission. No location or microphone permission is requested for this feature. Supported Android devices require Google Play services. iOS uses entered sleep duration or bedtime/wake time and the same optional daily check-in; no automatic Android Sleep API is claimed on iPhone.

Successful Android sleep segments are collected only after consent. Missing/low-confidence results are discarded. Raw estimates stay in app-private preferences, are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer, and are kept for up to seven days (at most fourteen segments). No raw segment is automatically uploaded. The morning screen offers **Confirm estimated sleep** and editable duration. Saving a confirmed estimate retains its timestamps and source `phone_estimate_confirmed`; editing its duration changes the source to `self_report`. Nothing is written to Health Connect or HealthKit.

Switching to a wearable or stopping tracking disables the receiver and removes unconfirmed estimates. Logout/account switching stops the old subscription and prevents callbacks from being attributed to a new account. An existing opt-in can resume on the same account without asking for permissions again. Permission denial/unavailable Play services still allows manual entry. Estimates start prospectively: yesterday's steps cannot reconstruct yesterday's sleep.

Daily check-in estimate requires reported sleep duration and all three answers: rest, energy and soreness. The transparent initial heuristic gives sleep duration 40% (capped at eight hours), rest 20%, energy 20%, inverse soreness 20%. It is labelled **Daily check-in estimate**, explained as a fitness estimate, and does not fabricate HRV, heart rate, sleep stages or sleep efficiency. It is not a validated physiological recovery calculation or personal-baseline model. Home uses a 0–10 API score displayed as 0–100. Valid device recovery takes precedence; the fallback never replaces a measured score. Recovery graphs continue to use device recovery; check-in scores are not mixed into that series.

Backend validates explicit NONE preference, bounded ratings/duration, estimate timestamps, duration consistency and wake-date timezone. Entries are scoped by account/date. Existing manual sleep and synced health records are preserved. Home cache version 11 clears the old response contract.

Validation: backend isolated integration tests cover UNKNOWN/WEARABLE rejection, NONE acceptance, switching back, device precedence, date/account isolation, provenance and invalid timestamps/duration. Flutter tests cover gating, no automatic permission request, Android opt-in, iPhone manual UI, confirmation/edit provenance and large text. Overnight sensor results require a physical Android device. iOS native build/review requires a Mac; App Store approval is not guaranteed by source changes.
